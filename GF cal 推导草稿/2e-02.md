

> **约定**: 前因子 $e^{\gamma_E\varepsilon}$ 自动消去所有 $\gamma_E$ 项，展开中 $\gamma_E$ 全部丢弃。

### 0.1 Wilson loop 的 C × J 结构

montopar 输出将每个 orbit 的贡献写为 $C \times J$：

$$C = C_F \cdot (4\pi\alpha_s) \cdot \frac{\Gamma(1-\varepsilon)}{4\pi^{2-\varepsilon}} \cdot (-2v_{14})^\varepsilon \cdot (-2)^{-1}$$

$$J(z) = P_z \cdot \int_{[0,1]^n} \prod f_i^{-1+\varepsilon} \, d^n x$$

### 0.2 2e-02 的拓扑

2e-02 是对边（opposite edge）型图。1-loop 四边形 Wilson loop 有两类 orbit：

| Orbit | 边类型 | 积分核结构 | $\varepsilon\to 0$ |
|-------|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| 2e-01 | 相邻 (cusp) | 单项式，因子化 | $1/\varepsilon^2$ 极点 |
| 2e-02 | 对边 (opposite) | 双线性 $B$ | **有限**，$=\mathcal{M}_{1,1}$ |

$\mathcal{M}_{1,1}$ 专属对边型，cusp 型不产生 $\mathcal{M}_{1,1}$。

### 0.3 α,ᾶ 表示

$$\alpha = \frac{1}{1+z}, \quad \bar\alpha = \frac{z}{1+z}, \quad \alpha+\bar\alpha=1, \quad z = \frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha}$$

$1+z = 1/\alpha$。在 α,ᾶ 表示下，$(1+z)$ 前因子被吸收，双线性核 $B$ 呈现齐次形式。

---

## 1. 参数化

### 1.1 montopar 输出



$$J_{02}(z) = -(1+z)\int_0^1 d\tau_1\int_0^1 d\tau_3 \; [(1-\tau_1)(1-\tau_3) + \tau_1\tau_3 z]^{-1+\varepsilon}$$


## 2. α,ᾶ 表示



用 $\alpha = 1/(1+z)$, $\bar\alpha = z/(1+z)$ 重写 $J_{02}$（记为 $J_A$）：

$$J_A(\alpha,\bar\alpha) = -\frac{1}{\alpha}\int_0^1 d\tau_1\int_0^1 d\tau_3 \; [(1-\tau_1)(1-\tau_3) + \frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha}\tau_1\tau_3]^{-1+\varepsilon}$$


## 3. 降维：2D → 1D

### 3.1 操作

$J_A$ 的被积函数对 $\tau_3$ 线性：

$$f(\tau_1, \tau_3) = (1-\tau_1)(1-\tau_3) + \frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha}\tau_1\tau_3 = A + B\tau_3$$

其中 $A = 1-\tau_1$, $B = \frac{\tau_1-\alpha}{\alpha}$。

### 3.2 基本积分

$$\int_0^1 (A+B\tau_3)^{-1+\varepsilon} d\tau_3 = \frac{1}{\varepsilon B}\left[(A+B)^\varepsilon - A^\varepsilon\right]$$

其中 $A+B = \frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha}\tau_1$。

### 3.3 结果

$$J_A = -\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\int_0^1 d\tau \; \frac{(\frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha})^\varepsilon \tau^\varepsilon - (1-\tau)^\varepsilon}{\tau - \alpha}$$

## 1. 起点: $J_A$ 的 1D 积分

$$J_A = -\frac{1}{\varepsilon} \int_0^1 \frac{\left(\frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha}\right)^\varepsilon \tau^\varepsilon - (1-\tau)^\varepsilon}{\tau - \alpha} d\tau$$

定义 $K_{tilde} = \int_0^1 \frac{(\frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha})^\varepsilon \tau^\varepsilon - (1-\tau)^\varepsilon}{\tau - \alpha} d\tau$，则 $J_A = -\frac{1}{\varepsilon}K_{tilde}$。

## 2. 交叉对称化 (1-loop)

$$\frac{\mathrm{sym}}{2}\cdot C(J(z)+z^{\varepsilon}J(1/z)) = C_F\left(\frac{\alpha_s}{\pi}\right)\left[(-2\pi v_{14})^\varepsilon + (-2\pi v_{12})^\varepsilon\right] \times I$$
$sym=2,v_{12}=zv_{14}$.

$J_A = J_B$（通过 $\tau \to 1-\tau$ 代换可证）。代入 $C = -\frac{1}{2}C_F\frac{\alpha_s}{\pi}\Gamma(1-\varepsilon)(-2\pi v_{14})^\varepsilon$：

$$-\Gamma(1-\varepsilon)J_A = \left[1+\left(\frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha}\right)^\varepsilon\right] I$$

$$I = \frac{\Gamma(1-\varepsilon)}{\varepsilon(\alpha^\varepsilon + \bar\alpha^\varepsilon)} \int_0^1 \frac{\bar\alpha^\varepsilon \tau^\varepsilon - \alpha^\varepsilon(1-\tau)^\varepsilon}{\tau - \alpha} d\tau$$

## 3. 变量代换 $\tau = 1-\beta$

$\tau-\alpha = (1-\beta)-(1-\bar\alpha) = \bar\alpha-\beta = -(\beta-\bar\alpha)$

$$I = -\frac{\Gamma(1-\varepsilon)}{\varepsilon(\alpha^\varepsilon + \bar\alpha^\varepsilon)} \int_0^1 \frac{(\bar\alpha\bar\beta)^\varepsilon - (\alpha\beta)^\varepsilon}{\beta - \bar\alpha} d\beta$$

## 4. 双曲分解

**分子**: $(\bar\alpha\bar\beta)^\varepsilon - (\alpha\beta)^\varepsilon = e^{\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln(\alpha\bar\alpha\beta\bar\beta)} \cdot 2\sinh\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln\frac{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}{\alpha\beta}\right)$

**分母**: $\alpha^\varepsilon + \bar\alpha^\varepsilon = e^{\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln(\alpha\bar\alpha)} \cdot 2\cosh\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln\frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha}\right)$

**相除** — $e^{\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln(\alpha\bar\alpha)}$ **对消**:

$$\frac{(\bar\alpha\bar\beta)^\varepsilon - (\alpha\beta)^\varepsilon}{\alpha^\varepsilon + \bar\alpha^\varepsilon} = e^{\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln(\beta\bar\beta)} \cdot \frac{\sinh\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln\frac{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}{\alpha\beta}\right)}{\cosh\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln\frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha}\right)}$$

**代回 I**:

$$I = -\Gamma(1-\varepsilon) \int_0^1 \frac{d\beta}{\beta-\bar\alpha} \cdot \frac{e^{\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln(\beta\bar\beta)}}{\cosh\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}L_d\right)} \cdot \frac{\sinh\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}L\right)}{\varepsilon}$$

其中 $L_d = \ln\frac{\bar\alpha}{\alpha} = \ln z$, $L = \ln\frac{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}{\alpha\beta}$。

## 5. 分离 $H(\varepsilon)$ 和 $V(\varepsilon)$

$$I = H(\varepsilon) \times V(\varepsilon)$$

$$H(\varepsilon) = \frac{-\Gamma(1-\varepsilon)}{2\cosh\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}L_d\right)}$$

$$V(\varepsilon) = \int_0^1 \frac{d\beta}{\beta-\bar\alpha} \cdot e^{\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln(\beta\bar\beta)} \cdot \frac{\sinh\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}L\right)}{\varepsilon/2}$$

## 6. 展开 $H(\varepsilon)$（丢弃 $\gamma_E$）

$$\Gamma(1-\varepsilon) = 1 + \cancel{\gamma_E\varepsilon} + \frac{\zeta_2}{2}\varepsilon^2 + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^3)$$

$$\frac{1}{\cosh x} = 1 - \frac{1}{2}x^2 + \mathcal{O}(x^4)$$

代入 $x = \frac{\varepsilon}{2}L_d$:

$$\frac{1}{\cosh(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}L_d)} = 1 - \frac{\varepsilon^2}{8}L_d^2 + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^4)$$

**关键**: $1/\cosh$ 是偶函数，展开从 $\varepsilon^2$ 开始，无 $\varepsilon^1$ 项。

$$H(\varepsilon) = -\frac{1}{2}\left[1 + \left(\frac{\zeta_2}{2} - \frac{L_d^2}{8}\right)\varepsilon^2\right] + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^3)$$

$$H^{(0)} = -\frac{1}{2}, \quad H^{(1)} = 0, \quad H^{(2)} = -\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\zeta_2}{2} - \frac{L_d^2}{8}\right)$$

## 7. 展开 $V(\varepsilon)$ — $\mathcal{M}_{k,1}$ 涌现

$$\frac{\sinh(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}L)}{\varepsilon/2} = L + \frac{\varepsilon^2}{24}L^3 + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^5)$$

$$e^{\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln(\beta\bar\beta)} = 1 + \frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln(\beta\bar\beta) + \frac{\varepsilon^2}{8}\ln^2(\beta\bar\beta) + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^3)$$

**相乘**:

$$V_{integrand} = L + \frac{\varepsilon}{2}L\ln(\beta\bar\beta) + \frac{\varepsilon^2}{8}L\ln^2(\beta\bar\beta) + \frac{\varepsilon^2}{24}L^3 + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^3)$$

**逐项积分**:

$$V(\varepsilon) = \mathcal{M}_{0,1} + \frac{\varepsilon}{2}\mathcal{M}_{1,1} + \frac{\varepsilon^2}{8}\mathcal{M}_{2,1} + \frac{\varepsilon^2}{24}\mathcal{M}_{0,3} + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^3)$$

其中:
- $\mathcal{M}_{k,1} = \int_0^1 \frac{d\beta}{\beta-\bar\alpha} L \ln^k(\beta\bar\beta)$ — 标准 M 基底
- $\mathcal{M}_{0,3} = \int_0^1 \frac{d\beta}{\beta-\bar\alpha} L^3$ — IBP 伴随积分

$$V^{(0)} = \mathcal{M}_{0,1}, \quad V^{(1)} = \frac{\mathcal{M}_{1,1}}{2}, \quad V^{(2)} = \frac{\mathcal{M}_{2,1}}{8} + \frac{\mathcal{M}_{0,3}}{24}$$

## 8. 母函数（通解）

### 8.1 $\Gamma(1-\varepsilon)$ 的 $\gamma_E$-free 展开

前因子 $e^{-\gamma_E\varepsilon}$ 消去全部 $\gamma_E$ 后：

$$\Gamma(1-\varepsilon) \to \exp\left(\sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{\zeta(k)}{k}\varepsilon^k\right) = \exp\left(\frac{\zeta_2}{2}\varepsilon^2 + \frac{\zeta_3}{3}\varepsilon^3 + \frac{\zeta_4}{4}\varepsilon^4 + \cdots\right)$$

### 8.2 完整的母函数

$$\boxed{I(\varepsilon) = -\frac{1}{2} \cdot \underbrace{\exp\left(\sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{\zeta(k)}{k}\varepsilon^k\right)}_{\text{$\zeta$ 生成子}} \cdot \underbrace{\operatorname{sech}\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}L_d\right)}_{\text{$\ln z$ 生成子}} \cdot \int_0^1 \frac{d\beta}{\beta-\bar\alpha} \cdot \underbrace{e^{\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln(\beta\bar\beta)}}_{\text{$\ln(\beta\bar\beta)$ 生成子}} \cdot \underbrace{\frac{\sinh\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}L\right)}{\varepsilon/2}}_{\text{$\mathcal{M}_{k,1}$ 生成子}}}$$

其中 $L_d = \ln z$, $L = \ln\frac{\bar\alpha\bar\beta}{\alpha\beta}$。

### 8.3 各级数展开

| 因子 | 级数展开 | 贡献 |
|------|---------|------|
| $\zeta$ 生成子 | $\displaystyle\exp\!\left(\sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{\zeta(k)}{k}\varepsilon^k\right) = 1 + \frac{\zeta_2}{2}\varepsilon^2 + \frac{\zeta_3}{3}\varepsilon^3 + \left(\frac{\zeta_4}{4}+\frac{\zeta_2^2}{8}\right)\varepsilon^4 + \cdots$ | $\zeta$ 值 |
| $\ln z$ 生成子 | $\displaystyle\operatorname{sech}\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}L_d\right) = \sum_{m=0}^\infty \frac{E_{2m}}{(2m)!}\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\right)^{2m} L_d^{2m}$ | $\ln^{2m} z$（偶次） |
| $\ln(\beta\bar\beta)$ 生成子 | $\displaystyle e^{\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln(\beta\bar\beta)} = \sum_{k=0}^\infty \frac{1}{k!}\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\right)^k \ln^k(\beta\bar\beta)$ | $\ln^k(\beta\bar\beta)$ |
| $\mathcal{M}_{k,1}$ 生成子 | $\displaystyle\frac{\sinh\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}L\right)}{\varepsilon/2} = \sum_{j=0}^\infty \frac{1}{(2j+1)!}\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\right)^{2j} L^{2j+1}$ | $L^{2j+1}$ |

其中 $E_{2m}$ 是 Euler 数：$E_0=1,\; E_2=-1,\; E_4=5,\; E_6=-61,\; E_8=1385,\;\ldots$

### 8.4 通项公式

$\varepsilon^N$ 阶的系数由 $N = 2m + k + 2j + \sum_p p \cdot k_p$ 的所有整数分拆给出（$m,k,j \ge 0$, $k_p \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \ge 2$）：

$$\boxed{I^{(N)} = -\frac{1}{2} \sum_{\substack{m,k,j \ge 0 \\ \{k_p\}}} \delta_{N,\, 2m+k+2j+\sum p k_p} \cdot \frac{E_{2m}}{(2m)!\,2^{2m}} \cdot \frac{1}{k!\,2^k} \cdot \frac{1}{(2j+1)!\,2^{2j}} \cdot \prod_{p\ge 2} \frac{\zeta(p)^{k_p}}{p^{k_p}\,k_p!} \cdot L_d^{2m} \cdot \mathcal{M}_{k,2j+1}}$$

其中 $\mathcal{M}_{k,2j+1} = \int_0^1 \frac{d\beta}{\beta-\bar\alpha} \cdot L^{2j+1} \cdot \ln^k(\beta\bar\beta)$。

- $j=0$: $\mathcal{M}_{k,1}$（标准 M 基底）
- $j>0$: $\mathcal{M}_{k,2j+1}$ 通过 IBP 归约到 $\{\mathcal{M}_{k+2j,1}, \mathcal{M}_{k+2j-2,1}, \ldots\}$ + $\zeta$ 项

### 8.5 前 3 阶的分拆

| $N$ | 分拆 $(m,k,j,\{k_p\})$ | 贡献 |
|-----|----------------------|------|
| $0$ | $(0,0,0,\{\})$ | $-\frac{1}{2}\mathcal{M}_{0,1}$ |
| $1$ | $(0,1,0,\{\})$ | $-\frac{1}{4}\mathcal{M}_{1,1}$ |
| $2$ | $(0,2,0,\{\})$ | $-\frac{1}{16}\mathcal{M}_{2,1}$ |
| | $(0,0,1,\{\})$ | $-\frac{1}{48}\mathcal{M}_{0,3}$ |
| | $(1,0,0,\{\})$ | $+\frac{1}{16}L_d^2 \mathcal{M}_{0,1}$ |
| | $(0,0,0,\{k_2{=}1\})$ | $-\frac{\zeta_2}{4}\mathcal{M}_{0,1}$ |

**$N=0,1$ 只有 $(j=0, m=0)$ 的分拆 → 纯 $\mathcal{M}_{k,1}$。$N \ge 2$ 开始出现 $\ln z$、$\zeta$、$W_j$ 的交叉项。**

## 9. $\ln z$ 项在 M 基底内的投影

### 9.1 $\ln^2 z$ 的 M 基底表示

$\mathcal{M}_{0,1}$ 的解析形式：$\mathcal{M}_{0,1} = -Hlog(z,[0,0]) - 3\zeta_2 = -\frac{1}{2}\ln^2 z - 3\zeta_2$

$$\boxed{\ln^2 z = -2\mathcal{M}_{0,1} - 6\zeta_2}$$

### 9.2 偶次 $\ln^{2m} z$ 的投影

$\ln^{2m} z$ 可通过 $\mathcal{M}_{0,1}$ 的幂次展开投影到 M 基底：

$$\ln^2 z = -2\mathcal{M}_{0,1} - 6\zeta_2$$
$$\ln^4 z = (\ln^2 z)^2 = 4\mathcal{M}_{0,1}^2 + 24\zeta_2 \mathcal{M}_{0,1} + 36\zeta_2^2$$
$$\ln^6 z = -8\mathcal{M}_{0,1}^3 - 72\zeta_2 \mathcal{M}_{0,1}^2 - 216\zeta_2^2 \mathcal{M}_{0,1} - 216\zeta_2^3$$

所有 $\ln^{2m} z$ 都在 $\{\mathcal{M}_{0,1}, \mathcal{M}_{0,1}^2, \mathcal{M}_{0,1}^3, \ldots\} \times \{\zeta_2, \zeta_2^2, \ldots\}$ 张成的空间内。

### 9.3 IBP 归约 $W_j$ 和 $\mathcal{M}_{k,2j+1}$

$$\mathcal{M}_{k,2j+1} = \int_0^1 \frac{d\beta}{\beta-\bar\alpha} \cdot L^{2j+1} \cdot \ln^k(\beta\bar\beta)$$

IBP 恒等式：$\frac{d}{d\beta}L = -\frac{1}{\beta\bar\beta}$

对 $j \ge 1$，可通过 IBP 将 $L^{2j+1}$ 降次，归约到 $\{\mathcal{M}_{k+2j,1}, \mathcal{M}_{k+2j-1,1}, \ldots\} \times \{\zeta_2, \zeta_2^2, \ldots\}$ 的线性组合。

**核心结论**：$\operatorname{sech}$ 只产生 $\ln^{2m} z$（偶次），全部可投影到 M 基底。$\sinh$ 产生 $L^{2j+1}$，$j=0$ 是 $\mathcal{M}_{k,1}$，$j>0$ 通过 IBP 归约。

**因此 $I(\varepsilon)$ 的所有 Laurent 系数都严格落在 M 基底张成的空间内。**

## 10. 最终结果

| 阶 | $I_{2e}^{(k)}$ | 纯 M 部分 |
|----|---------------|----------|
| $\varepsilon^0$ | $-\frac{1}{2}\mathcal{M}_{0,1}$ | $-\frac{1}{2}\mathcal{M}_{0,1}$ |
| $\varepsilon^1$ | $-\frac{1}{4}\mathcal{M}_{1,1}$ | $-\frac{1}{4}\mathcal{M}_{1,1}$ |
| $\varepsilon^2$ | $-\frac{1}{16}\mathcal{M}_{2,1} - \frac{1}{48}\mathcal{M}_{0,3} - \frac{\zeta_2}{4}\mathcal{M}_{0,1} + \frac{\ln^2 z}{16}\mathcal{M}_{0,1}$ | $-\frac{1}{16}\mathcal{M}_{2,1} - \frac{1}{8}\mathcal{M}_{0,1}^2 - \frac{1}{32}\mathcal{M}_{1,1} - \frac{11}{16}\zeta_2 \mathcal{M}_{0,1}$ |

## 11. 结构总结

$$\boxed{I(\varepsilon) = -\frac{1}{2} \cdot \exp\!\left(\sum_{k=2}^\infty \frac{\zeta(k)}{k}\varepsilon^k\right) \cdot \operatorname{sech}\!\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\ln z\right) \cdot \sum_{j,k\ge 0} \frac{(\varepsilon/2)^{k+2j}}{k!(2j+1)!} \cdot \mathcal{M}_{k,2j+1}}$$

**三个独立生成子的乘积**：
1. **$\zeta$ 生成子** — $\exp(\sum\zeta(k)\varepsilon^k/k)$，贡献纯 $\zeta$ 值
2. **$\ln z$ 生成子** — $\operatorname{sech}(\varepsilon\ln z/2)$，贡献 $\ln^{2m} z$（全部可投影到 $\mathcal{M}_{0,1}$ 的幂）
3. **$\mathcal{M}_{k,1}$ 生成子** — $\int d\beta/(\beta-\bar\alpha) \cdot e^{\varepsilon\ln(\beta\bar\beta)/2} \cdot \sinh(\varepsilon L/2)/(\varepsilon/2)$，贡献 $\mathcal{M}_{k,2j+1}$（$j=0$ 为标准 $\mathcal{M}_{k,1}$，$j>0$ 通过 IBP 归约）

**所有 Laurent 系数严格生存在 M 基底及其拓展空间内。**
