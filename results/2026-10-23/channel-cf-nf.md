# Ff: $C_F^2 T_F n_f$

The external factor is `lambda^3 * CF^2*TF*nf`. This page gives every coefficient from epsilon^-6 through epsilon^0 in the [common moment convention](conventions.md). It contains 2 orbit contributions.

## Finite result and anomalous dimensions

The renormalized finite coefficient is $F_{3,Ff}(L,\rho)$:

\[
\begin{aligned}& \frac{L^{3}}{12} - L^{2} \zeta\left(3\right) + \frac{55 L^{2}}{48} - \frac{L \rho^{2}}{16} - \frac{19 L \zeta\left(3\right)}{9} - \frac{\pi^{2} L}{8}\\ & {} - \frac{\pi^{4} L}{90} + \frac{1711 L}{432} + \frac{M_{1,1}}{8} - \frac{55 \rho^{2}}{192} + \frac{\rho^{2} \zeta\left(3\right)}{4} - \frac{55 \pi^{2}}{108}\\ & {} - \frac{355 \zeta\left(3\right)}{162} - \frac{14 \zeta\left(5\right)}{9} - \frac{19 \pi^{4}}{1620} + \frac{4 \pi^{2} \zeta\left(3\right)}{9} + \frac{42727}{7776}\end{aligned}
\]

Its scale-independent shape $f_{3,Ff}(\rho)=F_{3,Ff}(0,\rho)$ is:

\[
\begin{aligned}& \frac{M_{1,1}}{8} - \frac{55 \rho^{2}}{192} + \frac{\rho^{2} \zeta\left(3\right)}{4} - \frac{55 \pi^{2}}{108} - \frac{355 \zeta\left(3\right)}{162}\\ & {} - \frac{14 \zeta\left(5\right)}{9} - \frac{19 \pi^{4}}{1620} + \frac{4 \pi^{2} \zeta\left(3\right)}{9} + \frac{42727}{7776}\end{aligned}
\]

The cusp coefficient $k_{3,Ff}$ and non-cusp coefficient $h_{3,Ff}$ are, respectively:

\[
- \frac{55}{48} + \zeta\left(3\right)
\]

\[
- \frac{1711}{432} + \frac{\pi^{2}}{24} + \frac{\pi^{4}}{90} + \frac{19 \zeta\left(3\right)}{9}
\]

The contour anomalous dimension convention is $\gamma_{4,3,c}=2h_{3,c}$.
