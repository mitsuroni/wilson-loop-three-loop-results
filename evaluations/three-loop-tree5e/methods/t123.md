# Reassembling the T1, T2 and T3 three-loop families

This note follows the 31 ordered integrals in the T1/T2/T3 empty and
opposite-edge families from their accepted parameter-space roots to all seven
Laurent positions, from \(\epsilon^{-6}\) through \(\epsilon^0\).  The P6
calculation repeats the exact assembly from saved, evaluated HyperInt
primitives.  It does not repeat those integrations.  It does not take an old
physical-\(I\) coefficient table as an input, and it does not reconstruct
momentum-space Feynman rules.  The common domain is fixed real \(z>0\), with
real principal logarithms, followed by meromorphic continuation in
\(e=\epsilon\) to zero.  Formal proof status is `not_run`.

## The root and the physical normalization

Every root retains its original dependent endpoint bounds and its recorded
outer-to-inner order.  Write \(\kappa=1-2e\) and \(\ell=\log z\).  Before
using any saved primitive, `scripts/t123.py` checks that its native
`J_raw_s` equals the original ordered root, including the alpha measure and
order.  It also checks every multiplicative component of the native \(C\),
the orbit multiplicity four, the native assembly sign \(+1\), and the single
Euler compensation factor.  In particular, the phases and Fourier/coupling
constants reduce as

\[
i\,2\kappa\,\frac{iC_F}{4}\,
\frac{4^3}{4\cdot64}\,\left(-\frac12\right)
=\frac{\kappa C_F}{16}.
\]

This is a full-color statement.  The ordered color reduction
\(C_F=C_A^2C_F-2C_AC_F^2\), under
\(C_A^2=1+2C_AC_F\), is performed before selecting the first coordinate.
The raw coefficient of a literal color symbol by itself would not implement
this projection.  No sign repair is added by the reassembly adapter.

For all 31 orbits the resulting physical convention is

\[
G(e)=e^{-3\gamma_E e}\Gamma(1-e)\Gamma(1-2e),\qquad
I_g(z,e)=\frac{\kappa G(e)}8
\frac{J_g(z,e)+z^{3e}J_g(1/z,e)}{1+z^{3e}}.
\tag{1}
\]

The \(G\) here rewrites the compensation already present in \(C\); it is
not a second factor.  Equation (1) includes the orbit weight.  Thus neither a
reduced \(J\) nor a product of two lower-loop physical \(I\)'s can be silently
substituted for this \(I\).

## T1 with an adjacent pure line: the Gamma and convergent moments

The TGV has an isolated endpoint \(t\) on edge 3 and two endpoints
\(0<x<y<1\) on edge 4.  Put \(u=1-t\).  The source polynomials become

\[
S=zu\alpha_1(\alpha_2x+\alpha_3y),\qquad
P=\frac{z^2u}{4}\alpha_1(\alpha_2-\alpha_3).
\]

The simplex substitution
\(\alpha_1=a,\alpha_2=(1-a)b,\alpha_3=(1-a)(1-b)\)
has Jacobian \(1-a\).  Combining this Jacobian, numerator and denominator
before integrating makes the total \(a\) dependence \(a^{e-1}\).  The
integral is \(1/e\), leaving

\[
\frac{z^{2e}}{4e}u^{2e-1}
b^{-e}(1-b)^{-e}(2b-1)
[bx+(1-b)y]^{-2+2e}.
\tag{2}
\]

The original sorting of the additional adjacent endpoint then gives ordinary
power integrals.  With \(x=ry\), the only nontrivial two-variable weights are

\[
h_m(e)=\int_0^1dr\,db\,
r^{me}b^{-e}(1-b)^{-e}(2b-1)(1-b+br)^{-2+2e}.
\]

For 01--04 the complete roots are

\[
z^{-3e}(J_{01},J_{02},J_{03},J_{04})
=\left(\frac{h_0}{72e^5},
\frac{h_0-h_1}{36e^5},
\frac{h_0-h_1}{72e^5},
\frac{h_1}{72e^5}\right).
\tag{3}
\]

Define the analytic Gamma/Beta combinations

\[
A=\Gamma(1+e)\Gamma(1-e),\quad B=B(1-e,1-e),\quad U=2A-B,
\]
\[
V=\frac{\Gamma(1+e)\Gamma(1+2e)}{\Gamma(1+3e)},\qquad
W=\frac{\Gamma(1+e)^2}{\Gamma(1+2e)}.
\]

The known mother gives \(h_0=U/(\kappa e)\).  The other moment is reduced
by \(b=1/(1+t)\), then \(t=rv\), to

\[
h_1=\frac{B}{2e}-2F(e),\qquad
F(e)=\int_0^1dx\,dq\,
\frac{x^{1-e}(1-x)^{-e}(1-q)^{2e}}{1-xq}.
\tag{4}
\]

This is an evaluated special function if desired:

\[
F(e)=\frac{B(2-e,1-e)}{1+2e}
{}_3F_2(1,1,2-e;2+2e,3-2e;1).
\]

The saved `F0.txt` through `F5.txt` are its Taylor coefficients.  Their
defining logarithmic integral is

\[
F_q=\int_0^1dx\,dq'\,\frac{x}{1-xq'}
\frac{[-\log x-\log(1-x)+2\log(1-q')]^q}{q!}.
\]

For 17 the adjacent endpoint integration supplies \(1-V\).  For 18 and 19
the two neighboring sorting regions partition the 17 region.  The remaining
moment is

\[
Z(e)=\int_{[0,1]^3}\frac{x\,dx\,dr\,dy}{y(1-x+rx)}
e^{e[\log r-\log x-\log(1-x)+2\log y]}
[1-e^{e\log(1-ry)}],
\]
\[
Q(e)=\frac{B(1-2W+V)}{2e^2}-2Z(e).
\tag{5}
\]

The bracket in (5) cancels the apparent \(y=0\) pole.  Consequently
\(Z=\sum_{q=1}^4e^q Z_q+O(e^5)\); these are precisely `Z1.txt` through
`Z4.txt`, originally integrated in the order \(y,x,r\).  At the complete
epsilon level the same function can instead be given by the already
integrated convergent series

\[
Z(e)=-\sum_{k\geq1}\frac{(-e)_k}{k!(k+2e)}
\frac{B(2-e,1-e)}{1+e+k}
{}_3F_2(1,1,2-e;2+e+k,3-2e;1).
\]

It is not a finite Gamma expression.  The P6 output requires only its four
saved Taylor coefficients.  Combining (1)--(5), with
\(R=\cosh(e\ell/2)/\cosh(3e\ell/2)\), gives

\[
I_{01}=\frac{GU}{576e^6},\quad
I_{04}=\frac{\kappa Gh_1}{576e^5},\quad
I_{03}=I_{01}-I_{04},\quad I_{02}=2I_{03},
\]
\[
I_{17}=\frac{GRU(1-V)}{128e^6},\quad
I_{19}=\frac{\kappa GRQ}{64e^4},\quad I_{18}=I_{17}-I_{19}.
\tag{6}
\]

The Gamma/Beta jets through order six, \(F\) through five and \(Z\) through
four suffice for an \(O(e)\) remainder after (6).  Coefficients 01--04 are
independent of \(z\); 17--19 acquire only the even logarithms from \(R\).
The adapter imports the original `tgv-t1-empty/formulas.py` producer and
binds its moment reader to the fixed saved run.  Its exponential-jet
recurrence \(b_m=m^{-1}\sum_{k=1}^m k c_k b_{m-k}\) avoids generic expansion
of products of Gamma functions.

## T1 with an opposite pure line: two finite structure functions

Equation (2) still holds, but the pure line now has the positive-real
distance

\[
D_z(s,w)=(1-s)(1-w)+zsw
\]

and numerator \(-(1+z)\).  The sorting in 11 is \(x<w<y\), in 12 it is
\(w<x<y\), and in 32 the isolated endpoint contributes
\((1-w^{2e})/(2e)\).  The partition of the full \(x<y\) triangle implies
\(J_{11}+J_{12}=J_{32}\), hence the same full-\(e\) relation for \(I\).

Integrating the \(b,x,y\) triangle in 12 gives a numerator which vanishes
through order one:

\[
\mathcal N(e,w)=B\left[w^{2e}-1+2e(1-w)
{}_2F_1(1-2e,1-e;2-2e;1-w)\right]=O(e^2).
\]

Introduce

\[
\mathcal T=(1+z)\int_0^1ds\,dw\,D_z^{-1+e}(1-w^{2e}),\qquad
\mathcal K=(1+z)\int_0^1ds\,dw\,D_z^{-1+e}\mathcal N(e,w).
\]

For \(X=\mathcal T,\mathcal K\), set
\(\mathcal E_X=[z^{e/2}X(e,z)+z^{-e/2}X(e,1/z)]/2\).  Then

\[
I_{32}=-\frac{GU\mathcal E_{\mathcal T}}
 {128e^4\cosh(3e\ell/2)},\qquad
I_{12}=-\frac{G\mathcal E_{\mathcal K}}
 {128e^4\cosh(3e\ell/2)},\qquad I_{11}=I_{32}-I_{12}.
\tag{7}
\]

The actual finite integration route first integrates \(s\):

\[
(1+z)\int_0^1 D_z(s,w)^{-1+e}ds
=\frac{(zw)^e-(1-w)^e}{e[w-1/(1+z)]}.
\]

The interior pole is removable; its numerator difference must be retained.
The remaining \(b\) integration is organized into the four logarithmic
moments

\[
H_j(w)=\int_0^1db\,\frac{1-w}{1-b+bw}
\frac{[-\log b-\log(1-b)+2\log(1-b+bw)]^j}{j!},
\qquad 0\leq j\leq3.
\]

These temporary \(H_j\)'s are not the pre-existing single-opposite mother
\(H_0\).  The original HyperInt order was \(b\) then \(w\), with the final
fibration to \(z\) giving alphabet \(\{0,-1\}\).  `ET1..4.txt` and
`EK1..4.txt` store the fully integrated coefficients of
\(\mathcal E_{\mathcal T}\) and \(\mathcal E_{\mathcal K}\).
The former starts at order one; the latter at order two (`EK1` is exactly
zero).  They must be retained through order four in (7).  The analytic
multiplier therefore needs only order three.  The original formula module
performs this finite convolution and the ordered-domain subtraction in
exact shuffle arithmetic.

## T2: a source reflection, not a new integration

The T2 roots are mapped to T1 by exchanging edges 1/2 and 3/4, reversing
every edge's endpoint order, sending its endpoint coordinate to
\(1-s'\), and swapping \(\alpha_1,\alpha_3\) while fixing
\(\alpha_2\).  The absolute Jacobians are one and \(z\) is unchanged.
This is a bijection of the dependent ordered domains, preserving the full
numerator, denominator, pure-line factor and normalization.  Thus the
full physical equalities used here are

\[
(I_{05},I_{06},I_{07},I_{08},I_{20},I_{21},I_{31})
=(I_{04},I_{03},I_{02},I_{01},I_{19},I_{18},I_{17}),
\]
\[
(I_{16},I_{36},I_{37})=(I_{32},I_{12},I_{11}).
\tag{8}
\]

These relations preserve the original multiplicity; each source still has
its own input binding and output record.  The portable adapter's root check
retains the entire native ordered \(J\) before reusing (8).  The package's
independent source controls separately test the polynomial/domain maps.

## T3 with an adjacent line: pushing the ordered triangle to one variable

For T3 put

\[
F_z(t,u)=z(1-t)(1-u)+tu,\qquad A_z(t)=(1+z)t-z.
\]

This \(F\) is related to the previous distance by \(F_z=zD_{1/z}\), and
must not be identified with \(D_z\).  The root is

\[
S=\alpha_1[\alpha_2F_z(t,x)+\alpha_3F_z(t,y)],\qquad
P=-\frac{1+z}{4}A_z(t)\alpha_1(\alpha_2-\alpha_3).
\]

The same simplex substitution integrates its scale, while the adjacent
pure endpoint gives the weights

\[
W_{09}=1-y^e,\qquad W_{10}=y^e-x^e,\qquad W_{26}=1-t^e.
\]

The additional line therefore weights the original T3 kernel; multiplying
a lower-loop physical \(I\) would miss these ordering factors.  To remove
the same-edge triangle, push \(u=bx+(1-b)y\) forward and denote the
densities for weights \(1,y^e,x^e\) by \(\rho_0,\rho_y,\rho_x\).
Their derivatives, retaining both contact faces, are

\[
\rho'_0=(T_e-B)/e,\quad
\rho'_y=(T_e-Bu^e)/e-\mathcal A_e,\quad
\rho'_x=(\mathcal Q_e-Bu^e)/e,
\]
\[
T_e=[u(1-u)]^{-e},\quad
\mathcal A_e=u^e\int_u^1v^{-1-2e}(1-v)^{-e}dv,
\]
\[
\mathcal Q_e=\Gamma(1-e)\Gamma(1+e)
\left[{}_2F_1(1+2e,-e;1;u)
+2eu\,{}_2F_1(1+2e,1-e;2;u)\right].
\]

The densities vanish at the two endpoints.  The identity
\(A_zF_z^{-2+2e}=-\kappa^{-1}\partial_uF_z^{-\kappa}\)
then permits an integration by parts without discarding a contact face.
Define

\[
L_9=B(u^e-1)+e\mathcal A_e,\qquad
L_{10}=T_e-e\mathcal A_e-\mathcal Q_e,
\]
\[
Z_e(z,u)=\frac{u^{2e}-[z(1-u)]^{2e}}
 {2e[u-z/(1+z)]},
\]
\[
H_9=\int_0^1Z_eL_9\,du,\quad
H_{10}=\int_0^1Z_eL_{10}\,du,
\]
\[
H_{26}=(1+z)\int_0^1dt\,du\,
F_z(t,u)^{-\kappa}(1-t^e)(T_e-B).
\]

The apparent interior pole of \(Z_e\) is again removable.  Both \(L_9\)
and \(L_{10}\) start at order two; the two weights of \(H_{26}\) each
start at order one.  A useful endpoint-subtracted representation for the
\(\mathcal Q_e\) part is

\[
\mathcal R_e(u)=\int_0^1\frac{dv}{v}
\left[\frac{1-2uv}{1-uv}
e^{e[\log(1-v)-2\log(1-uv)-\log v]}-e^{-e\log v}\right],
\quad L_{10}=T_e-1-e\mathcal A_e+e\mathcal R_e.
\]

The saved run first expanded and integrated these subtracted
\(\mathcal A,\mathcal R\) moments.  It then integrated \(v,u\) for 09/10
and \(t,u\) for 26.  With

\[
\mathcal E_g=\tfrac12[z^{-3e/2}H_g(z)+z^{3e/2}H_g(1/z)]
=e^2E_{g,2}+e^3E_{g,3}+e^4E_{g,4}+O(e^5),
\]

the fully evaluated `E9q2..4`, `E10q2..4` and `E26q2..4` give

\[
c_{g,-2}=-E_{g,2}/32,\qquad c_{g,-1}=-E_{g,3}/32,
\]
\[
c_{g,0}=-\frac1{32}
\left[E_{g,4}+\left(\frac52\zeta_2-\frac98\ell^2\right)E_{g,2}\right].
\tag{9}
\]

All more singular stored positions are zero.  Reflection of every edge
coordinate, \(b\mapsto1-b\) and \(z\mapsto1/z\) maps
39 to 09, 38 to 10, and 30 to 26.  At the raw level this is
\(J_{\rm reflected}(z)=z^{3e}J(1/z)\); equation (1) converts it into
\(I_{39}=I_{09}, I_{38}=I_{10}, I_{30}=I_{26}\).

## T3 with an opposite line: two ordered double kernels and one product

For 22--25 the pure line has kernel
\(-(1+z)F_z(p,q)^{e-1}\).  The ordered domains are

| Orbit | First edge | Same edge as \(x,y\) |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | \(t<p\) | \(x<y<q\) |
| 23 | \(t<p\) | \(x<q<y\) |
| 24 | \(p<t\) | \(x<q<y\) |
| 25 | \(p<t\) | \(q<x<y\) |

Let \(\rho_0\) be the unweighted T3 density above.  The low and high
triangle densities at fixed \(q\) are
\(q\rho_0(u/q)\mathbf1_{u<q}\) and
\((1-q)\rho_0((u-q)/(1-q))\mathbf1_{u>q}\).
The middle density is their subtraction from \(\rho_0\).  The endpoint
zeros remove additional delta contact terms when differentiating.  After
the same boundary-preserving IBP, the weights are

\[
W_{\rm low}=T_e(u/q)-B\quad(u<q),
\]
\[
W_{\rm mid}=\begin{cases}
T_e(u)-T_e(u/q),&u<q,\\
T_e(u)-T_e((u-q)/(1-q)),&u>q.
\end{cases}
\]

Both start at order one.  The 22 structure is

\[
\mathcal H_{22}=(1+z)^2\int_{t<p}\!dt\,dp\int_{u<q}\!du\,dq\,
F_z(t,u)^{-1+2e}F_z(p,q)^{-1+e}W_{\rm low}.
\]

For 23/24, the complementary \(t,p\) domains form a square before the
physical symmetrization.  Define

\[
Z_\delta(z,u)=\frac{u^\delta-[z(1-u)]^\delta}
 {\delta[u-z/(1+z)]},\qquad
\mathcal K=\int_0^1du\,dq\,Z_{2e}(z,u)Z_e(z,q)W_{\rm mid}.
\]

The physical structures are
\(\mathcal E_{22}=[z^{-3e/2}\mathcal H_{22}(z)
+z^{3e/2}\mathcal H_{22}(1/z)]/2\) and
\(\mathcal E_{23}=z^{-3e/2}\mathcal K\).  Thus

\[
I_{22}=\frac{G\mathcal E_{22}}{32e^2\cosh(3e\ell/2)},\qquad
I_{23}=\frac{G\mathcal E_{23}}{64e^2\cosh(3e\ell/2)}.
\tag{10}
\]

Reflection gives the full equalities \(I_{25}=I_{22}\) and
\(I_{24}=I_{23}\).  The saved `E22q1..2` and `E23q1..2` are the
fully integrated first two coefficients of these structures.  Since
\(G/\cosh=1+O(e^2)\), division by 32 or 64 gives the simple pole and
finite term directly; positions -6 through -2 are zero.  The original 22
route integrated \(p\), then \(t,u,q\); 23 first combined the complementary
\(t,p\) domains.  Neither route treats a removable pole as a new singular
letter of the final answer.

Orbit 44 separates in the raw domain: its T3 factor is in the \(F\)
frame and its pure factor in the \(D\) frame.  The bridge
\(S_F=zS_D(1/z), P_F=z^2P_D(1/z)\), with the retained simplex and
ordering, must be applied before using a known mother.  In terms of the
existing single-opposite mother \(H_0(e,z)\), put

\[
\mathscr C(e,z)=\frac{\pi}{e\sin\pi e}
[\cos\pi e-\cosh(e\ell)],\qquad
\Delta=\mathscr C-BH_0(2e,z).
\]

The raw factors are
\(J_{\rm T3}=z^e\Delta/(4\kappa e^2)\) and
\(J_{\rm pure}=z^{e/2}H_0(e,z)\).  Consequently

\[
I_{44}=\frac{GH_0(e,z)\Delta}{32e^2\cosh(3e\ell/2)}.
\]

With the existing generators \(M_j=M_{j,1}(z)\),

\[
H_0(e,z)=M_0+\tfrac e2M_1+O(e^2),\qquad
\Delta=-e(2M_0+M_1)
-e^2[(4-\zeta_2)M_0+2M_1+M_2/2]+O(e^3).
\]

Their product yields the complete nonzero coefficients

\[
c_{44,-1}=-\frac{M_0(2M_0+M_1)}{32},\qquad
c_{44,0}=-\frac{(4-\zeta_2)M_0^2+3M_0M_1
+(M_0M_2+M_1^2)/2}{32}.
\tag{11}
\]

The adapter obtains \(M_0,M_1,M_2\) from their own saved raw Hlog outputs
`M_0_1.txt`, `M_1_1.txt`, `M_2_1.txt` and multiplies them by the shuffle
identity.  It avoids the old unified table which also contains final target
answers.  Equation (11) is an existing-family representation and is also
expanded into complete Hlog coordinates in the output.

The output is a finite exact rational vector in words
\(G(a_1,\ldots,a_n;z)\), \(a_i\in\{0,-1\}\), multiplied by powers of

\(dt/(t-a)\); in particular \(G(0;z)=\log z\) and
\(G(-1;z)=\log(1+z)\).  Even zeta values are reduced to this period
basis, and all products are expanded with exact shuffle multiplicities.
An empty term list is the exact zero coefficient.  No unevaluated
structure integral in this note is used in place of a coefficient.

of already evaluated Hlog vectors, not new integrations or new function
families.  P6's primitive-to-vector assembly does not use those old target
fits to produce its answers.  Any later adopted compact view must be
checked by expansion into the regenerated vector; the complete coordinates
already constitute the explicit analytic coefficients.
