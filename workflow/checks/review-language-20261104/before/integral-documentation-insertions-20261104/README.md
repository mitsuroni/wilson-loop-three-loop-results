## Findings and difficult steps

The full opposite Pi1 numerator tends to `(1-2e)z`, not zero, at its zero corner. A draft assertion `|N|<=C D` was false. The corrected full density is bounded by `C D^(-2+2Re e)`; with the two-dimensional measure its radial integral is `r^(-1+2Re e)dr`, integrable strictly on the positive starting strip. Holomorphy of the weaker weighted H body is proved separately. This concrete mathematical correction matters even though all saved coefficients already matched.

P3 retains its weighted derivative boundary/Beta mass; P5 retains all eight inner/outer rectangle terms and uses the mixed T first derivative only to the required order; P6 retains its independent-square derivative corner mass. The article-specific maps justify physical-I aliases without silently equating all bare J functions. Historical pre-migration scalar signs were excluded from current normalization.

The chain and Pi2 bodies use the complete transverse tensor and all four opposite corners. They share endpoint J but have different native scalar signs and different outside operators. Pi2 retains its actual AA/Af/Ff channels; the serial chain has AA/Af/ff. The fifth-order inverse-U coefficient is required before the regular Pi2 multiplier is truncated to order four. Source operators, geometric I and full colours are never identified with one another.

The new data indices distinguish mathematically needed moments from functions loaded globally by a batch, and identify adopted internal operators separately from whole-I comparison targets. The generator reads no complete-I reference or old operator-expansion table. Its actual role is source checks plus established analytic reductions, Gamma/moment expansion and complete operator convolution, without a fresh CAS integration.
