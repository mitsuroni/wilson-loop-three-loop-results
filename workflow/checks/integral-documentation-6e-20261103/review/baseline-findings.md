## Actual implementation examined

The immutable `engine/wilson_calc/assembly.py` consumes the prepared Euler factor once and uses `kappa=sym*a1/[2*(-2)^n]`. `exchange.py` reconstructs the ordered endpoint chains without graph-number lookups. `ordered_backend.py` separates an all-opposite q=0 root calculation from the two-opposite normalized-gap q=0…2 calculation. In the latter, its returned M excludes the explicit `(1+z)^2*z^(r*epsilon)/epsilon^2` prefactor; it does not equal the old centered V by default.

The batch's `scripts/primitive_replay.py` reads archived raw coefficient text only after matching the current mathematical request, script, order inventory and successful old record, and reparses it before fresh assembly. This is a legitimate explicit adoption, not a new Maple integration. Script/hash matching establishes identity of the reused objects; it cannot replace the missing derivation of their connection to the source or independently prove the old integration.

The final review will examine every new own article and its shared mathematical appendix, compare their actual code/data roles, and report per-entry conclusions. It will preserve exact full-J/full-I/finite-only equality scopes. Neither seven matching saved coefficients nor automatic format/link checks will be accepted as proof of a stronger identity. Real z>0, local epsilon continuation, empirical numerical controls and `formal=not_run` remain distinct from arbitrary physical complex-z or z=-1 claims.
