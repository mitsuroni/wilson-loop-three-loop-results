# Fixed standard1.0: lessons from the39-entry6e trial

This is an independent forward reading review, with the original P2 articles and research/code examined before the new articles. The standard remained fixed. The reviewer did not author the new mathematical methods, recompute any original integral, launch a cold CAS, run a formal proof, or alter the scientific selections. The final per-entry assessment is in [audit.json](audit.json).

## Shared derivations can be exact without hiding each graph

The ten adjacent-only cases do not need ten copies of the same Beta proof. A single evaluated ordered-word lemma suffices once every own article identifies its actual inequalities, endpoint letters, exact word product, z power, kappa and coefficient substitution. The new articles do this and expose the degree-six regular jet needed by the apparent sixth-order pole. Equal chain products with different color/orbit normalization remain separate source contributions.

The fifteen one-opposite cases are a stronger test. Their different endpoint placements yield distinct complete weights; 30/31 retain an incomplete BA insertion in the unsymmetrized J. A proved reflection identity removes it only in the physical-I pairing with equal phases. The new shared text derives the exact Mellin-Barnes/Meijer-G source and the positive-quadrant Jacobian, logarithm kernel and actual integration order. Its thirty explicit evaluated HLOG jets make the fixed-order answer concrete; naming tau/eta alone would not do so.

The remaining fourteen cases require their own adopted objects to be typed mathematically. The actual four-variable gap M excludes `(1+z)^2*z^(r*epsilon)/epsilon^2`. The older centered V includes other factors, and the relation between their first two nonzero coefficients includes a logarithmic scale correction. The five all-opposite outputs instead are ordinary original J0, with the original numerator and dependent bounds. Neither object is an already normalized final I. The articles and complete raw/reflected appendices now preserve those distinctions.

## Concrete issues found and resolved

- A draft common recurrence defined an unpaired centered B but used its coefficients without the physical average. It now explicitly defines coefficients of the full paired source, including any remaining phase. The source-specific finite formulas retain the crossing-odd term.
- Normalized endpoint powers A_t/B_t/A_s/B_s were initially denoted by bare AT/AS names near formulas also using endpoint densities A(x)/B(x). Their mathematical definitions now distinguish the two roles.
- The common compact-function navigation initially omitted E's precise definition. It now supplies the actual ordered two-kernel logarithm insertion and its evaluated dictionary, alongside the ordinary, non-factorial tau_D4 convention.
- The shared thirty-jet appendix initially had the wrong relative base for its raw-output links. All thirty were corrected. Fourteen original-order/inner-order TeX strings also joined `\to` to `s` as the undefined command `\tos`; their separators were corrected.
- An entry with no newly consumed primitive event could still use many cached moments. The data indices initially omitted such transitive dependencies. All39 now index the actual required objects, with first-replay owner/cache status, exact raw paths/hashes/order/type and explicit path bases. This gives544 per-entry references to174 distinct raw files, not544 integrals.

No mathematical result was changed to match an old answer. These findings concern exposition, precise coefficient extraction, implementation scope and reproducible data lineage. The full source and result histories remain frozen.

## Exact output checks have a useful, limited role

The reviewer independently parsed the new mathematical expressions/tables and their linked native raw outputs. All94 expressions and1425 nonzero word/period coordinates agree exactly. This establishes transcription of the stated adopted evaluations, rather than scientific completeness by heading count or a numerical fit. The [check script](check_hlog_transcription.py) and [actual output](hlog-transcription-check.json) preserve that limited claim.

The root integrator separately reexecuted the actual copied engine/provider:39 sources,273 coefficient positions and273 function back-substitutions passed; ten Gamma expansions and69 archived primitive events were used with zero new external CAS. The isolated generation started without final references and reproduced all39 coefficient payloads. The complex-route author also checked23 nontrivial physical coefficients with another finite assembly over the same raw primitives. Those are distinct execution and algebra checks, not three independent evaluations of the original integrals.

Only20/21/22/37/38 received the new direct ordinary finite-source numerical controls. Integer-regulator polynomial comparisons on seven representatives control their source/domain operations at those values; they do not determine Laurent finite coefficients for the other34 graphs. Preserving this limit is part of the standard.

## Equality scope is mathematical content

The exact source maps prove the complete-I aliases10/23,11/25,12/24 and21/22, without declaring their original unreflected J equal. The32/34 relation has the stronger original-J factor-exchange proof. The37/38 relation is finite-only: their different kappa and Q kernels prevent a complete-I identity. The regular-kernel test and the exact reduced difference1161/266240 make that failure concrete without substituting an integer regulator into Gamma at a pole. Agreement of the seven saved slots would not prove any stronger relation.

## Decision

Retain standard1.0 unchanged. Efficient batch treatment comes from proving shared lemmas once, displaying every own substitution, and adopting evaluated objects with exact conditions and results. It does not require39 new integrations or artificial files in three languages. It also does not permit unexplained JSON reductions, final-table readers, missing endpoint constants or stronger aliases inferred from matching coefficients.

The final finding is ten directly continuous articles and twenty-nine continuous articles with explicit evaluated adoptions, with no unresolved document/implementation gap after the listed corrections. This remains research-level mathematics on fixed real z>0 with local epsilon continuation; arbitrary physical complex branches, direct z=-1 boundaries, basis minimality and integral-level formal proof are not granted by the review. No Git/publication action was performed.
