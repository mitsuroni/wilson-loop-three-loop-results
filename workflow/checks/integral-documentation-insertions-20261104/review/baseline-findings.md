## Actual implementation and adoption scope

The frozen scripts pi1.py and chains_pi2.py generate coefficients without opening old complete-I tables. They adopt already evaluated M HLOG coordinates and, for the coupled Pi1 route, diagonal ordered rho0/rho1 coordinates. The Pi2 full-D internal operator is an explicitly adopted literature/native input. Neither script runs a new Maple/Wolfram computation. Declared mathematical source matches and parser success identify adopted objects; they do not prove the original integrations.

The Pi1 code's paired_kernels reconstructs the six specified expressions, expands Gamma/rational factors, and restores the complete regular external B1. It preserves the distinction between the unprojected CF(CF-CA/2) trace and the connected a1=-1/2 contribution. The serial branch restores B1 squared and therefore AA/Af/ff; Pi2 restores its own AA/Af/Ff scalar operator. Scalar/geometric I, external operator and complete colored coefficients must remain separate.

The chain/Pi2 implementation explicitly checks its original numerator, mixed derivative, four ordinary opposite corners, one Euler compensation and the native residual signs. It generates U and inverse U through order five, removes the spurious phi_-1 coefficient, and checks both phi times U-containing geometry and Uphi times U-free geometry. These operations are stronger than selecting a prior expansion table, but share the same adopted internal operator and M dictionary.
