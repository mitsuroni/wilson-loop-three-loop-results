## A proof-condition correction that replay alone could not find

The draft Pi1 convergence argument bounded the full opposite numerator by a constant times D. But its actual numerator is `-2(1+z)D+(1-2e)z`, which tends to a nonzero constant at the zero corner. The claimed bound was false even though the eventual integrability conclusion on the positive strip was true.

The corrected absolute kernel bound is `C D^(-2+2Re e)`. In two dimensions, D is comparable to the sum of the distances and the radial model is `r^(-1+2Re e)dr`, which is integrable for strictly positive Re e. The weighted H body's weaker power allows its separate local holomorphy argument; that argument cannot be silently transferred to the full divergent tensor root. The reviewer sent the exact error to the author and checked the corrected shared and own text.

This correction illustrates why the standard asks for operation conditions and a continuous source connection. Saved coefficients, hashes or a passing evaluator cannot validate a new explanatory bound that the evaluator does not use.
